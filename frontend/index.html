Step-by-Step Procedure

1

Discharge Power Supply Capacitors

Unplug all power cables. Use a 1kΩ 5W resistor across main capacitor terminals to discharge residual high voltage safely.

2

Desolder Damaged IC

Apply fresh flux to all 3 pins. Heat with soldering iron at 350°C and suction away solder with desoldering pump.

3

Clean Pads & Install Replacement

Clean copper pads with 99% IPA. Apply thermal paste to new IC backplate and solder firmly to PCB.

4

Verify Voltage Rail

Power on device and measure output with multimeter. Confirm steady 5.0V DC at ±4% tolerance under full load.
